# Feeding and Nutrition

General guidance on building feeding into a daily care schedule. Portion sizes
and diets should be confirmed with a veterinarian.

## Consistent meal timing
Pets thrive on predictable meal times. Feeding at roughly the same times each day
stabilizes digestion, supports house-training, and reduces begging and anxiety.
When you must change a feeding time, shift it gradually over several days rather
than all at once.

## Spacing meals across the day
Two meals a day are typically spaced 8 to 12 hours apart (for example, morning
and early evening). If a pet eats three or more meals, spread them evenly through
the waking hours. Avoid feeding a large meal immediately before or after
vigorous exercise, which can increase the risk of digestive problems.

## Transitioning foods
When switching to a new food, transition gradually over 7 to 10 days by mixing an
increasing proportion of the new food into the old. A sudden switch commonly
causes stomach upset. Slow transitions give the digestive system time to adjust.

## Treats and portion control
Treats should make up no more than about 10 percent of a pet's daily calories to
avoid weight gain and nutritional imbalance. Measure meals with a proper cup or
scale rather than estimating, and adjust portions based on body condition and a
veterinarian's guidance rather than the bag's default range alone.

## Fresh water
Clean, fresh water should be available at all times and refreshed daily. Wash
water bowls regularly. Reduced water intake or sudden increased thirst can signal
a health problem and warrants a veterinary check.
